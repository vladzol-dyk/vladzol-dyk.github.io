Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Content OS Publisher ("the app") is a private tool used only by its owner to manage their own YouTube channel.

Data the app accesses

  • Permission to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel.
  • Read-only access to the owner's channel details and video statistics (views, likes, comments, analytics).

How the data is used

Data is used only to publish the owner's videos and to measure how they perform. It is stored on the owner's own computer and private server, is never sold, and is never shared with third parties.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is accessed through the YouTube API Services, subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored data is deleted on request.

Contact

Use the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
