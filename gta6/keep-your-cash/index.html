How to Keep Your Cash in GTA VI

Most early money problems come from losing cash you already earned. Build these habits first.

Pocket cash is at risk

If you die or get arrested, the cash you are carrying is lost. Store it after each job instead of carrying it into the next one.

Use the floorboards, then BuckMe

Before your bank account opens, use the floorboard stash in Jason's bedroom. After the Buck Me mission, deposit at an ATM.

  • Deposit before a risky job
  • Check your mailbox after story missions
  • Sell loot before it can be lost

Fund Family Savings before Chapter 1 ends

Guides report that pocket cash, the floorboard stash, and your main bank balance do not carry into Chapter 2, while Family Savings does. Aim for at least $5,000 there.

Keep a manual save

Chapters move you forward, so make a manual save in a new slot for each chapter.
