How to Sell Stolen Cars in GTA VI

Car selling becomes useful after Chapter 1. The key is checking the tracker before you drive anywhere.

Scan first with WAiNK

Use WAiNK before taking a car. It shows the car's security, sale value, and registration cost, so you can skip cars your current buyer will not accept.

Match the car to the buyer

Guides generally report this buyer order. Unlock timing and payouts can differ, so trust your in-game prompt.

  • Moses: cars with no tracker, starting in Chapter 2
  • Pay 'n' Spray: no tracker or standard tracker, usually from Chapter 3
  • Randy: expensive cars with advanced trackers, later in the story

Avoid the common mistakes

Do not steal right beside Moses's garage, and avoid unnecessary damage because it can lower the price. His garage can also need a short wait between sales.

Bank after each sale

Do not carry a growing pile of cash from one car to the next. Deposit it first, because money in your pocket can be lost if you die or get arrested.
