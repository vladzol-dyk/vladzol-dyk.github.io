How to Lose the Cops in GTA VI

Police work from clues. The fastest escape is often changing what they know, not just driving faster.

Remove the clues

Handle the details police can identify when they are not watching you.

  • Change your outfit
  • Put visible weapons away
  • Ditch or switch your vehicle
  • Destroy CCTV evidence when possible
  • Split up if you are with a partner

Protect stash bags

Stash bags can be lost if you die. Bags left in a car can also be lost if the car is impounded, so sell valuable loot soon after a job.

Plan before bigger jobs

Know your exit before entering. Bigger robberies bring much heavier police pressure, and a large take is worth nothing if you lose it during the escape.
