Broke to Bank

GTA VI Treasure Diving for Early Money

Treasure diving is one of the safer early ways to earn because it does not depend on robbing stores.

How it starts

Guides report that diving opens through Kenton after the Island Dreams mission in Chapter 1. You get access to his boat, scuba gear, and a metal detector.

Use the metal detector

Equip the detector, aim it, and follow the signal. Stay close to the target so you do not lose the signal, then collect the treasure.

  • Return to the boat when you need air
  • Watch for sharks
  • Bring recovered treasure back to Kenton

What it can pay

Guides report that dives can pay a few thousand dollars and improve as you do more. Exact payouts vary, so treat any number as an estimate.

Protect the money

After Kenton pays you, store or deposit the cash before starting another risky job. Before Chapter 1 ends, make sure enough is in Family Savings.
